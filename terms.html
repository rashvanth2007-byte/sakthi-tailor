Terms, Refund & Cancellation Policy

This legal page is available in English only.

Last updated: 20 October 2026. Items marked in italics are to be confirmed by the shop owner.

Placing an order

Orders sent through the website's WhatsApp form, by phone or in person are confirmed only after the shop confirms the design, measurements, price and delivery date with you.

Measurements & fabric

Garments are stitched to the measurements taken at the shop or the sample garment you provide. Please check the fabric you hand over; the shop is not responsible for colour bleeding or shrinkage of fabric it did not supply.

Advance payment

To be confirmed: whether an advance is required, and whether it is refundable before cutting begins.

Cancellations

You can cancel free of charge before your fabric is cut. Once cutting or stitching has started, the order cannot be cancelled.

Alterations & rework

If the fit is not right because of a stitching error, the shop will alter it free of charge. Please try the garment on at pick-up and tell us about any issue within 7 days (to be confirmed). Changes requested to the original design or measurements may be charged.

Pick-up & delivery

The shop will tell you when your order is ready. If a delivery date is likely to be missed, the shop will contact you on WhatsApp or by phone as early as possible. Please collect finished orders within 30 days (to be confirmed).

Uniform & bulk orders

Bulk orders from schools, offices and institutions are confirmed with a written quote listing quantity, sizes, model, price and delivery schedule.

Sewing machine sales

New machines carry the manufacturer's warranty where applicable. Used machines: return/repair policy to be confirmed.

Training school

Course fee, refund and certificate terms to be confirmed at enrollment.

Contact for disputes

Phone / WhatsApp: 97896 51814. We will always try to resolve any issue directly and fairly.
