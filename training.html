Sakthi Tailoring Training School

Learn tailoring from working professionals.

Hands-on classes taught at our own shop — from basic cutting to bridal jackets, at your own pace.

Enroll via WhatsApp See what you'll learn

Who it's for

A practical course for everyone

  • Beginners

    Never used a machine? Start with the basics — threading, straight seams and measuring.

  • Homemakers

    Stitch blouses and chudidhars for your family and save on tailoring costs.

  • Future tailors

    Build the skills to start your own tailoring business from home or a shop.

Curriculum

What you'll learn

Cutting

  • Taking body measurements
  • Sadha blouse and chudidhar cutting
  • Lining blouse cutting
  • Princess cut
  • Two- and three-piece katori cutting

Stitching & finishing

  • Boat neck, collar neck and closed neck
  • Chudidhar and blouse finishing
  • Aari work and machine embroidery basics
  • Model and bridal jackets
  • Saree frock, zig-zag and pico

Real tools

Tools you'll work with

The same pattern-drafting tools and machines used at the shop every day.

Student work

Made by our students

Pages from our students' practice albums: patterns, small garments and embroidery stitches, all made by hand in class.

Course notes

Every cut, drawn and explained

Students get hand-drawn cutting drafts in Tamil with every measurement formula, so you can practise at home.
