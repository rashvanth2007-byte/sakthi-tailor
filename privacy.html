What this website collects

The "Order or Book a Service" form on this website asks for your name, phone number, the service you need, and any details you choose to add. When you press "Send via WhatsApp," this information is placed into a WhatsApp message and sent directly from your device to the shop's WhatsApp number. This website does not have a server or database, so it does not itself store, process, or transmit your form data anywhere else.

Cookies

This site sets one small preference cookie (stored in your browser) to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice. No advertising or analytics cookies are used. You can clear this preference at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
