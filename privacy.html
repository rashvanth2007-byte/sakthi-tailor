Privacy Policy

This legal page is available in English only.

Last updated: 20 October 2026. This is a plain-language policy for a small business website; it is not legal advice.

Who we are

This website belongs to Sakthi Tailor & Garments (the "shop"), a tailoring business with two branches in Tamil Nadu, India. Phone / WhatsApp: 97896 51814.

What this website collects

The "Order or book a service" form asks for your name, phone number, the service you need, your preferred branch, an optional date and any details you choose to add. When you press "Send via WhatsApp", this information is placed into a WhatsApp message on your own device. Nothing is sent until you press send inside WhatsApp, and it goes only to the shop's WhatsApp number.

This website has no server-side database. It does not store, sell or share your form details, and it does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

What we store on your device

  • Language choice (sakthi_lang) — remembers the language you picked. Essential for the site to work the way you asked.
  • Privacy choice (sakthi_cookie_choice) — remembers whether you allowed Google Maps.
  • Offline copy — a service worker saves the main pages so the site still opens with a weak signal.

These are stored in your browser's local storage, never sent to us, and you can remove them at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Third-party services

  • Google Maps — the branch map is only loaded if you choose "Show map" or "Allow maps". Google may then set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy. "Directions" links open Google Maps directly.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are downloaded from Google's servers, which see your IP address as part of a normal web request.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook — links open those services, which are governed by Meta's own privacy policies.

How the shop uses your enquiry

Messages you send on WhatsApp are used only to reply to you, take your order, and arrange measurement, trial and delivery. Order details kept by the shop (such as measurements) are used only for your orders and are not shared with anyone else.

Your rights under the DPDP Act, 2023

You can ask the shop to:

  • Tell you what personal data it holds about you and why
  • Correct or update inaccurate data
  • Erase data that is no longer needed for your orders
  • Stop using your data, by withdrawing consent as easily as you gave it

If you are not satisfied with the shop's response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Grievance contact

Contact the shop owner on 97896 51814 (call or WhatsApp), or speak to us at either branch. We aim to respond within 7 days.

Changes to this policy

If the website adds new features (such as online payments), this page will be updated and the date above changed.
