Questions

Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Fill in the order form on this page — it opens WhatsApp with your details ready to send. You can also call 97896 51814 or walk into either branch.

Do you take bulk uniform orders for schools and offices?

Yes. We stitch school and office uniforms in all models, for individuals as well as institutions. Share the quantity and model on WhatsApp for a quote.

How long does stitching take?

It depends on the design and how busy the season is. We confirm the delivery date with you when we take your measurements.

Do you buy and sell used sewing machines?

Yes. We sell new machines and buy and sell used ones. Send the machine model and a photo on WhatsApp.

Can I learn tailoring with you?

Yes. Sakthi Tailoring Training School teaches cutting and stitching hands-on at the shop. See the Training School page for details.
